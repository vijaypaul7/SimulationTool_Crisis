2.2 Leakage Audit
Identified Leakage: Disclosed in results/manifest.json:

"Behavioural variables (fundamentalist_weight, extrapolator_weight, behavioural_response) were computed by the ABM using fuel.shift(-1) as the realised price target. This embeds mild look-ahead in the rolling MSFE."
Partial Mitigation in Code: In modules/forecasting_models.py, behavioural variables receive an extra lag (.shift(lag)) when merging into feature sets.

Leakage-Free Baselines: The AR-only and Energy+AR models do not utilize ABM behavioural variables and are completely free from look-ahead leakage.
